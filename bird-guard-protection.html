Why it matters

The gap between your roof and your solar panels is a near-perfect nesting spot: sheltered, elevated, and hard to reach. Pigeons find it fast, and once they're in, the problems compound.

Nesting material and droppings build up under the panels, adding weight and mess that's difficult to clean without removing the panels themselves.

Birds and rodents drawn to nests can chew through wiring, a real fire risk and a common cause of unexplained system faults.

Droppings and debris on the panels themselves mean more frequent cleanings just to keep output where it should be.

Left long enough, nesting damage can lead to repairs that cost far more than prevention would have.

What's included

An on-site inspection to measure the perimeter of your array and check for existing nesting or damage.

Custom-fit, rust-proof galvanized steel mesh, installed to seal the gap without drilling into or voiding your panel warranty.

Removal of any existing nesting material before the guard goes in, not sealed in place over it.

Often bundled with a solar panel cleaning or free roof inspection visit to save you a second trip.
