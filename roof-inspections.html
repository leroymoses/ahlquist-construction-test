Why get an inspection

Arizona roofs take a beating from UV exposure, monsoon storms, and extreme summer heat. Most damage doesn't show up as a leak until it's already been building for a while. An inspection catches it early, when the fix is small.

Before monsoon season, to catch weak points before storms find them.

After a storm, to check for wind or hail damage you can't see from the ground.

Before buying or selling a home, for a clear picture of the roof's real condition.

If you're planning solar installation or panel cleaning and want the roof itself checked first.

What we check

Shingle, tile, or membrane condition and wear.

Flashing, seals, and vents for gaps or deterioration.

Signs of water intrusion, sagging, or prior repair work.

Overall structural condition, with a clear summary of anything worth addressing.

No Cost

Free, always.

There's no charge for the inspection and no obligation to book repair work with us afterward. You'll get a plain-language summary of what we found and, if anything needs attention, an honest recommendation on next steps.
