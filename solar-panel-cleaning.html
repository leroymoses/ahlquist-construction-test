Why panels need regular cleaning here

Phoenix gets less than 8 inches of rain a year, which means nothing is naturally rinsing dust, pollen, or mineral buildup off your panels. Left alone, that buildup quietly cuts into the production you're paying your system to deliver.

Dirty panels typically lose 15–25% efficiency in the Valley's dust and heat.

Monsoon dust storms can coat a full array in a single afternoon.

Hard water leaves mineral spotting that builds up over multiple seasons if untreated.

Most homeowners see the best results cleaning twice a year — after monsoon season and before peak summer output.
